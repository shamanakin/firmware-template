# NEXUS · FIRMWARE — the learned regulatory layer

> The adaptive layer **between** DNA (fixed identity) and expressed behavior. Holds learned calibrations, context-gating rules, working memory — the marks that modulate *how* the DNA expresses, without changing the identity underneath. Updated continuously (where DNA is slow and deliberate); inherited across sessions (this is what lets a new session resume *how* to operate, not just *who* ⟨YOUR-NAME⟩ is).

## Role (three-layer model)

DNA (genome, slow) → **FIRMWARE (regulatory, fluid, inherited)** → phenotype (the session's actual output).
